# Privacy

_Last updated: October 13, 2026_

Ledger keeps your financial data on your iPhone. It has no account, no server and no network
access, so the developer never receives any of your data.

## What Ledger stores

- The accounts, categories, transactions, opening balances, notes and reference numbers you
  enter.
- Your display and logging preferences.

When you use **Log From Message**, Ledger reads the text it's given on the device to find the
amount, the direction and a reference number. The message itself isn't saved. Only the transaction
you confirm, with its reference number, is.

## Where it's stored

- In a database inside Ledger's private app container on your iPhone, protected by iOS Data
  Protection.
- In your iCloud Backup or computer backup, if you've turned those on. Ledger has no sync and no
  other copy.
- Deleting the app deletes its data.

## What Ledger doesn't do

- It makes no network requests, and has no sign-in, server or bank connection.
- It has no analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party code.
- It doesn't read your notifications, contacts, location or photos.
- Its diagnostic logs never contain amounts, balances, account or category names, notes or
  reference numbers.

## Shortcuts, Siri and the Action Button

Ledger's actions run on your iPhone and require it to be unlocked, because they read or change
financial data. If you use Siri, Apple handles your voice request under
[Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
